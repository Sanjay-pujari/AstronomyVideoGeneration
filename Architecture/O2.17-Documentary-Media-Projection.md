# O2.17 Documentary Media Projection

O2.17 projects retained O2.16 certified payload material into four deterministic documentary variants. Facts are accepted only from explicit semantic-fact objects and are filtered by topic applicability; a shared payload can therefore contain multiple topics without leaking unrelated facts into a projection.

The constellation and planet-conjunction topic families use distinct deterministic profiles. Constellations require identity, visibility, a major feature, and an observation or location fact. Planet conjunctions require event identity, involved objects, a date or time window, angular separation, visibility or direction, and observation guidance. Each profile also owns its long-form ordering and short-form priorities.

Subtitle segmentation preserves word order and produces as many one- or two-line cues as necessary. The cue set covers the complete narration without dropping words, respects configured line limits, and spans the narration duration with ordered, nonoverlapping offsets.

Narration, subtitle, and visual knowledge references are validated against the retained certified payloads. Validation checks payload identity and type, source and artifact identity/version, correlation, canonical owner-local sequence, JSON-pointer syntax, and pointer resolution.

This is a focused O2.17 completion pass submitted for architectural review. It is not an O2.17 certification and does not begin O2.18.
